# Lab 02 - Microsoft Teams Access and Basic Support

## Objective

The objective of this lab is to practise basic Microsoft Teams support tasks from an IT Support and Service Desk perspective.

This lab focuses on reviewing Teams access, checking team members, reviewing channel settings, creating a support channel, posting a test message, and checking channel details.

## Lab Environment

| Component | Details |
|---|---|
| Platform | Microsoft Teams |
| Team Used | IT Support Labs |
| Channels Used | General, Support Requests |
| Role Practised | Entry-level IT Support / Service Desk technician |

## Tasks Completed

- Opened Microsoft Teams in the browser
- Opened the IT Support Labs team
- Reviewed the General channel
- Reviewed team members and roles
- Reviewed General channel settings
- Created a new standard channel called Support Requests
- Added a channel description for IT support communication
- Posted a test message in the Support Requests channel
- Reviewed Support Requests channel details
- Captured screenshots for documentation

## What I Learned

In this lab, I learned how to review a Microsoft Teams team from a support perspective.

I practised checking team membership, reviewing channel settings, creating a support channel, and posting a test message. I also learned how Teams channels can be used to organise support communication and troubleshooting updates.

## IT Support Relevance

Microsoft Teams is commonly used for workplace communication and collaboration.

Service Desk technicians may need to support users who cannot access a team, cannot see a channel, need to be added to a team, or need help understanding where to post support requests.
