## What I Learned

I learned how to organise collaboration support scenarios into Service Desk tickets and a standard troubleshooting workflow.

The project helped me understand the importance of confirming user identity, reviewing permissions, documenting troubleshooting steps, protecting user data, and verifying resolutions.

## IT Support Relevance

Service Desk technicians support users across multiple collaboration applications.

A consistent workflow helps technicians identify problems, perform first-line checks, escalate complex issues, and document outcomes.
