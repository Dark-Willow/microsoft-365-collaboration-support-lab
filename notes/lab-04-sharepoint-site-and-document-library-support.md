## What I Learned

I learned how to navigate SharePoint sites, review permissions, and manage files within document libraries.

I also gained an understanding of how SharePoint access can be granted through site membership, groups, and direct file sharing.

This is important when troubleshooting users who cannot open documents or have incorrect access permissions.

## IT Support Relevance

Service Desk technicians may receive requests involving:

- Users unable to access SharePoint sites
- Missing documents or folders
- Incorrect file permissions
- Problems sharing documents
- Users who require view-only or editing access

Understanding SharePoint permissions helps technicians investigate access issues and escalate complex permission problems appropriately.
